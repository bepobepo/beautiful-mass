Questions

The things people
ask first.

Why is it free?

Because I’d rather show you this is useful than tell you. I’m building this practice in the non-profit space, and two hours of my time is a fair price for finding out whether AI is worth yours. There’s nothing to sign and no obligation at the end.

Do we need to be technical?

No. Most of the organisations this is built for have nobody in a technical role, and that’s the point. If you can describe how your week works, we have enough to start.

What if we have no budget and no CRM?

That’s the usual starting position. The consultation, the website update, and the simple CRM are free, and anything we set up is chosen so a small organisation can actually keep running it.

What do you do with our data?

In the consultation I only need to understand how you work, not to see your records. If we go further and I do need access to something real, we agree in writing what I can see before I see it.

What happens after the two hours?

You keep the written summary and priorities, and you’re free to stop there and run with it yourself. If you’d like help putting it in place, that’s a separate conversation we’d both have to agree to — the free session isn’t a sales meeting in disguise.

Are we too small for this?

Small is where this works best. Fewer people means fewer approvals, so a change you decide on this week is a change you are actually running next week. There’s no minimum size or budget to book a call.
