Privacy

Beautiful Mass is run by Benjamin Poliak, based in London.

What I collect. If you book a consultation, I collect the name, email address and anything you write in the booking form. Bookings are handled by Google Calendar. If you email me, I hold that email.

What I use it for. Arranging and preparing for the consultation, and following up afterwards. Nothing else. I don’t sell it, share it, or add you to a mailing list.

Your organisation’s data. The consultation doesn’t require access to your records. If later work does, we agree in writing what I can access before I access it.

How long I keep it. I keep booking and email records only while I still need them to follow up, then delete them. You can ask me to delete them sooner.

Your rights. You can ask me what I hold about you, ask me to correct it, or ask me to delete it — email benji@beautifulmass.ai and I’ll do it within 30 days. If you’re unhappy with how I’ve handled your data you can complain to the ICO at ico.org.uk.
